
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \methodname{}, a teacher-student framework that learns a reusable 3D morphing function from pseudo-trajectories generated by a training-free model. Operating directly on 3D latent representations, our approach combines a learned dual-source gating mechanism with a frozen generative prior to improve both transition regularity and intermediate quality. We also introduced Source Profile Error (SPE), a metric for evaluating perceptual progression relative to both source objects. Experiments demonstrate more consistent transitions, competitive visual quality, and faster generation compared to existing approaches. Furthermore, directly conditioning on 3D representations reduces the dependence on the viewpoints used for morphing initialization. These results highlight the potential of learning coherent and efficient 3D morphing instead of relying on current training-free approaches.
